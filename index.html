Bounce Court

Keep the ball in the circle.

Swipe a paddle around a round court, keep an ever-faster ball in play, and smash the jelly bricks in the middle. Every stage lays the bricks out a new way, every fifth brings a boss, and after each one you pick a perk that lasts the whole run. You have three hearts. Make them count.

How it plays

  1. Swipe the track. Along the bottom of the screen is the court unrolled into a straight line. Swipe and the paddle follows your thumb around the rim. Lift and put your thumb down anywhere and the paddle stays put. Hold an end and it keeps turning, all the way round.
  2. Break the middle. Jelly bricks orbit the centre. Every brick you break before the ball comes back to the paddle raises your multiplier; touch the paddle to bank it. A lollipop doubles the chain.
  3. Clear the stage. Break enough bricks and the board pops. The next stage comes in a new layout, with new kinds of brick as you go, and a boss every fifth stage.
  4. Pick a perk. After every stage, choose one of three: a longer paddle, a twin paddle, a magnet, zaps, blasts and more. They stack for the whole run, so no two runs play alike.
  5. Keep up. The ball speeds up the whole time. Miss and you lose a heart; lose all three and the run is over. Beat a boss and your next runs can start right after it.
  6. Chase the board. Your score goes on a Google Play Games leaderboard, with thirteen medals to collect, missions to complete and a new daily challenge every day, with its own twist and its own board.

Bricks with a trick

  • Armour breaks only when you hit it from the outside.
  • Bomb blows up the bricks around it.
  • Blinker fades out now and then. Hit it while it's solid.
  • Golden runs along its ring and is worth five bricks.
  • Reverser flips the way the bricks spin.
  • Wall never breaks. Bank shots!
  • Boss every fifth stage: a core behind a spinning shield. Beat it for a checkpoint.
